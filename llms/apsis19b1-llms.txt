# Apsis

> Apsis One is an AI-driven, all-in-one marketing platform combining email marketing, SMS, marketing automation, event management, forms, surveys, website tracking, and an AI Assistant for email content. Founded in Sweden in 2001 and now part of the efficy Group, it is GDPR-compliant and ISO-certified, with all data stored and processed within the EU, and is trusted by over 3,600 businesses across the Nordics and Europe.

Notes for interpreting this site:

- Apsis One is the current, recommended platform. Apsis Pro is the original, still-supported email platform for existing customers.
- Apsis One integrates with CRM systems including Tribe, efficy CRM, Lime CRM, Microsoft Dynamics, webCRM, and Salesforce, and connects to 5,000+ apps via Zapier. An open API is available.
- The AI Assistant supports eight languages, keeps all data in Europe, and never stores, shares, or trains on prompt data.
- A 14-day free trial with full platform access is available, no credit card required.
- The site is available in English, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, and Finnish.
